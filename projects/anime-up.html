Anime Up

Are you an anime fan, tired of opening app after app to check whether the new episode is out? Just ask Alexa.

Alexa skill Data updater

What it does

Anime Up is an Italian-language Alexa skill that gives you live information about the anime currently airing: what comes out today or on a given day, which episode a series has reached, when the next one is out, the plot, and even whether it's worth starting.

The skill is under active development and is tested on the Alexa development stage. It isn't published in the Alexa Skills Store yet.

What you can ask

What's out today

"Che anime ci sono oggi?" ("Which anime are on today?"), or any weekday or date: "Cosa esce di venerdì?"

Latest episode

"A che episodio siamo arrivati?" ("Which episode are we at?") and "Quando esce?" ("When is it out?").

Plot & advice

"Qual è la trama di…?" ("What's the plot of…?") and "Me lo consigli?" ("Would you recommend it?").

A conversation

You: apri anime up

Alexa: Ciao, mio piccolo nerd preferito, che vuoi sapere?

"Hi, my favourite little nerd, what do you want to know?"

You: quali anime escono oggi

Alexa: Oggi, ci sono in programma le uscite di: …

"Today, the following releases are scheduled: …"

How it works

  1. Collect: a Python updater queries the AniList GraphQL API for the anime currently airing around the world, caching results locally to avoid being rate limited.
  2. Filter & store: the list is filtered and normalised, then saved to MongoDB (a Docker Compose setup is included) or to a JSON file.
  3. Answer: the Alexa skill runs as an Alexa-hosted AWS Lambda function in Python, matches what you ask to one of its intents and answers from the collected data.
  4. Ship: the skill manifest, interaction model and Lambda are deployed together with the ASK CLI, with separate development and live stages.
